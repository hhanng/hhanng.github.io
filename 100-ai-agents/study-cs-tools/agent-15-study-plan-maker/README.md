# Study Plan Maker

**Category:** Study & CS

Enter your exam date, subject, and available hours per day and get a color-coded day-by-day study calendar built specifically around your schedule.

## Features

- Color-coded daily calendar that balances topics across the days until your exam
- Breaks large subjects into manageable daily chunks with built-in review days
- Adjusts automatically if you change your exam date or daily availability
- Downloadable or printable calendar view

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser (or visit the live demo on the portfolio).
2. Enter your Gemini API key in the settings panel.
3. Enter your exam date, subject or list of topics, and how many hours per day you can study.
4. Click **Build Plan** to generate the calendar.
5. Review the day-by-day schedule and print or screenshot it for offline use.

## Tech Stack

- HTML / CSS / JavaScript (vanilla)
- [Google Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/) (`gemini-2.0-flash`)
- No build step — open and go
