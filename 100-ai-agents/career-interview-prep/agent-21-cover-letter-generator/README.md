# Cover Letter Generator

A tailored cover letter writer that reads the job description, matches your background, and delivers a polished 3-paragraph letter — powered by the Gemini API.

Part of the [hhanng.github.io](https://hhanng.github.io) agent portfolio · Career

---

## What It Does

- Accepts company name, role, job description, your background, and preferred tone as inputs
- Generates a clean 3-paragraph cover letter aligned to the specific role
- Highlights matched JD keywords in yellow directly in the letter text
- Displays keyword chip badges so you can see at a glance how well your letter aligns

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser (or visit the live demo from the portfolio)
2. Fill in the company name, role title, and paste the job description
3. Add a brief background summary (skills, experience, achievements)
4. Choose your tone (professional, enthusiastic, conversational, etc.)
5. Click **Generate Cover Letter**
6. Review the letter — yellow highlights show matched keywords; copy and paste into your application

## Tech Stack

- HTML / CSS / Vanilla JavaScript
- [Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/) (gemini-2.0-flash) for letter generation and keyword extraction
- No build step — runs entirely in the browser
