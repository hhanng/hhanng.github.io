## 📋 Description
10 modules covering every social situation for Vietnamese teens at a US high school — fully bilingual EN/VI.

## ✨ Features
- 10 modules: First Day, Making Friends, Classroom, Lunch, Humor, Hard Situations, Extracurriculars, Dating, Social Media, Talking to Adults
- 200+ scenarios with EN phrases, pronunciation guides, and Vietnamese translations
- Cultural tips and "what NOT to say" guides
- Daily phrase of the day + progress tracker + quiz mode
