Why Build in Public?

👁️
Recruiter visibility
Recruiters at FAANG actively look for candidates who demonstrate skills publicly. Your GitHub + social presence IS your portfolio.
🔗
Network compounding
Each post connects you with people. Those people refer you, answer your questions, hire you. The network builds on itself.
📊
Public accountability
Announcing what you're building holds you to it. Consistent output compounds over time.
✅
Portfolio proof
Show don't tell. 100 public commits + build threads = stronger signal than any resume bullet.
