Savings & Emergency Fund

401(k) — Your Most Powerful Tool

A pre-tax (or Roth) retirement account through your employer. Contributions reduce your taxable income right now.

2024 Limit
$23,000
Employer Match (typical)
3–6%
💡 Golden rule: Always contribute at least enough to get the full employer match. That's a 50–100% instant return.

Traditional 401k

  • Pre-tax contributions
  • Pay tax on withdrawal
  • Best if: you expect lower income in retirement
  • Required minimum distributions at 73

Roth 401k

  • After-tax contributions
  • Tax-FREE withdrawals
  • Best if: you're in a low bracket now (new grad!)
  • No RMDs

RSU Tax Guide

RSUs are taxed as ordinary income when they vest — based on the stock price at vest date.

ScenarioTax TreatmentRisk
Sell immediately at vestIncome tax onlyNone
Hold < 1 year after vestIncome tax + short-term cap gainsMedium
Hold > 1 year after vestIncome tax + long-term cap gains (lower!)High (stock volatility)
⚠️ Watch out: RSU income is added to your W-2 and may push you into a higher bracket. Your employer withholds ~22% but you may owe more at tax time.
