Privacy Policy

Food Match, including its Who's Free feature (the "App"). Effective November 12, 2026.

Food Match helps friends see who is free and pick a place to eat together. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have. The App is made by Han Han, an individual developer ("we", "us"). Questions: ngvuhhan07@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Phone numberTo create your account and sign you in with a one-time code. Friends can add you by this number.
Display nameThe name your friends see. You choose it when you set up your profile.
Friend codeA short random code we generate for your invite link.
Friends and requestsWho you added, who added you, and pending requests, so the App can show your friends.
"I'm free" statusWhether you said you are free and until when. Your accepted friends can see it.
Swipe roomsRooms you create or join, who is in them, your likes, passes and vetoes, and the resulting match.
Location (only when you use it)When you create a room or tap "Surprise me", the App asks for your location to find restaurants nearby. We use it to search, and a room keeps the coordinates you searched from.
Blocks and reportsWho you blocked, and reports you send about other users (the reason and any note you add), so we can keep the App safe. Only we can read reports; the person you report is not told who reported them.
Push notification tokenSo we can tell you when a friend is free, sends a request, or invites you to a room. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings.

We do not access your contacts, photos or microphone. We do not show ads, we do not sell your information, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use information

  • To run the App: sign you in, show your friends, share your "free" status with accepted friends, and run swipe rooms.
  • To send notifications you can switch off at any time.
  • To keep the App safe and prevent abuse, for example by reviewing reports and limiting how many phone-number lookups can be made in an hour.

3. What other people can see

  • Your display name is visible to your friends, to people with a pending request with you, and to people in a room with you.
  • Your "free" status is visible only to your accepted friends.
  • Your phone number is never shown to other users. Someone who already knows your number can send you a friend request by typing it; if the number has no account, they are only told that nobody was found.

4. Service providers who handle data for us

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Twilio: sends the one-time sign-in code by text message. We only send sign-in codes, never marketing. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Google (Places API): receives the search location and filters to return nearby restaurants and photos.
  • Expo and Apple: deliver push notifications to your device.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account information until you delete your account. Room data is kept only as long as needed to run the feature. When you delete your account, your profile, phone number, friendships, friend requests, "free" status, push token, invites, and rooms you created or joined (including your votes), and the people you blocked are deleted. Reports you sent about others may be kept so we can finish reviewing them, but they are no longer linked to your account.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: in the App, go to Home, then Account, then Delete account. This is permanent.
  • Change your name or remove a friend: inside the App. You can also email us.
  • Location and notifications: allow or deny them in iOS Settings; the App still works without notifications.
  • Block or report someone: tap "Report or block" next to a person on the Friends screen or in a swipe room. Blocked people can no longer find you, add you or join your rooms, and you can unblock them on the Friends screen.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or Texas), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information. Use the in-App delete option or email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

The App is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access rules so that people can only read what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Where data is processed

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. By using the App you understand your information may be transferred there.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the App.
