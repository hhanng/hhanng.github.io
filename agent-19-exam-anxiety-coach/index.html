Thought Reframing

Your anxious thoughts aren't facts. Let's flip them into something helpful.

"I'm going to fail."
→
"I've studied and prepared. Even if it's hard, I can work through each question one at a time."
"I'm going to blank on everything."
→
"My brain stores more than I think. If I blank, I'll skip and come back — the answer often surfaces."
"Everyone else is smarter than me."
→
"I only see other people's preparation, not their struggles. I have my own strengths and knowledge."
"This exam will ruin my future."
→
"One exam is one data point. My future is built on patterns of effort, not a single moment."
"I haven't studied enough."
→
"I've done what I could with the time I had. Worrying now won't add knowledge — staying calm will help me access it."
