# Research Topic Finder

A smart research assistant that generates tailored academic topic ideas based on your subject, paper type, and difficulty level — powered by the Gemini API.

Part of the [hhanng.github.io](https://hhanng.github.io) agent portfolio · Study & CS

---

## What It Does

- Takes your subject area, paper type (argumentative, analytical, etc.), and difficulty level as inputs
- Generates 6 distinct research topics, each with a clear thesis statement
- Provides supporting arguments and a structured outline for every topic
- Helps you move from a blank page to a solid research direction in seconds

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser (or visit the live demo from the portfolio)
2. Enter your subject area (e.g., "Machine Learning", "Environmental Policy")
3. Select your paper type and difficulty level
4. Click **Generate Topics**
5. Browse the 6 topic cards — each includes a thesis, key arguments, and an outline
6. Copy whichever topic fits your assignment

## Tech Stack

- HTML / CSS / Vanilla JavaScript
- [Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/) (gemini-2.0-flash) for topic generation
- No build step — runs entirely in the browser
