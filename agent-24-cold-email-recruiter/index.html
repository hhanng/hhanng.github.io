💡 Tips for Sending

📅

Best Days

Send on Tuesday or Wednesday — recruiters are least overwhelmed mid-week. Avoid Mondays and Fridays.

⏰

Best Times

Aim for 8–9 AM or 1–2 PM in the recruiter's time zone to land at top of inbox.

🔁

Follow-Up

Wait 5–7 business days before following up. Use the Follow-Up tab above to generate your sequence.

🔗

LinkedIn First

Connect on LinkedIn before emailing — a shared signal boosts your reply rate.

📎

Attachments

Don't attach your resume in the first email. Link to your LinkedIn or portfolio instead.

✂️

Keep It Short

If your email is over 150 words, cut it. Recruiters spend <10 seconds on a cold email.
