💡 Tips for Sending

📅

Best Days

Send on Tuesday or Wednesday — recruiters are least overwhelmed mid-week. Avoid Mondays (inbox overload) and Fridays (wind-down mode).

⏰

Best Times

Aim for 8–9 AM or 1–2 PM in the recruiter's time zone. Emails land at the top of the inbox when they start their day or after lunch.

🔁

Follow-Up

Wait 5–7 business days before following up. Keep it to one sentence: "Just checking in — still very interested in [Role] at [Company]."

🔗

LinkedIn First

Connect on LinkedIn before emailing — a shared connection signal boosts your reply rate. Use the email as a "follow-up to my connection request."

📎

Attachments

Don't attach your resume in the first email. Link to your LinkedIn or portfolio instead — attachments trigger spam filters and feel presumptuous.

✂️

Keep It Short

If your email is over 150 words, cut it. Recruiters spend <10 seconds on a cold email. Hook them in the first line or lose them forever.
